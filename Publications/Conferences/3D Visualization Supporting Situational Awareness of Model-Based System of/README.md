# Title

3D Visualization Supporting Situational Awareness of Model-Based System of Systems

# DOI

https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-19-3610-4_8

# Conference

International Symposium on Knowledge and Systems Sciences

# Authors

Yuqian Cao, Boyu Xie, Jian Wang, Xiaochen Zheng, and Jinzhi Lu

# Abstract

Model-based System of Systems Engineering (MBSoSE) enables to improve the efficiency of the architecture design, and the performance analysis and modeling of highly complex system of systems (SoS). Introducing situational awareness into complex system of systems engineering (SoSE) can facilitate users to understand and analyze the SoS models and make decisions through
the situational awareness. However, conventional texts or models cannot be intuitively understood by users through traditional SoSE approach. Therefore, this paper proposes a 3D visualization technology to support situational awareness using MBSoSE. A semantic modeling solution based on KARMA language and Graph-Object-Port-Property-Relationship-Role (GOPPRR) ontology is proposed to combine 3D visualization technology with co-simulation to enhance situational awareness through enhanced vision. It can enhance the capabilities of perception, understanding and prediction of decision makers. A case study is conducted to prove the effectiveness of the proposed solution which the braking scene is modeled in an intelligent vehicle SoS using the 3D visualization technology to
dynamically co-simulate the braking scene.

# Keywords

MBSE , KARMA , 3D visualization , Situational awareness
